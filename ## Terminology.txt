## Terminology

- **Role Silo (RS):** A bounded operational context with explicit capability allowlists and refusal rules.
- **ECAS:** Explicit Contextual Authority Switching; the protocol governing role transitions.
- **EMI:** ECAS Modal Intercept; a blocking consent interface.
- **CSO:** Consent State Object; a signed, immutable record of user consent.
- **BEL:** Behavioral Enforcement Layer; runtime policy enforcing silo constraints.
- **CRE:** Conflict Resolution Engine; adjudicates policy conflicts.
- **AITX:** Federated governance infrastructure providing identity, policy, audit, and conflict resolution.
