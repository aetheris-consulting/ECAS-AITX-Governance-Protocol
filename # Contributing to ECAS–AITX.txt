# Contributing to ECAS–AITX

Thank you for your interest in contributing.

This repository defines a **governance protocol**, not a product implementation.
Contributions should preserve clarity, scope discipline, and technical rigor.

---

## Contribution Guidelines

### What We Welcome
- Clarifications that improve implementability
- Corrections to technical or architectural inaccuracies
- Additional diagrams or test guidance
- Security or threat-model improvements
- Editorial improvements that increase precision

### What We Do Not Accept (v1)
- Feature expansion beyond defined scope
- Emotional companionship or UX ideology debates
- Regulatory guarantees or legal claims
- Model training prescriptions
- Autonomous weapons discussions

---

## Process

1. Open an issue describing the change
2. Reference the affected RFC section(s)
3. Explain impact on conformance or threat model
4. Submit a focused PR

Large changes may be deferred to v1.1+.

---

## Review Criteria

Changes are evaluated on:
- Technical correctness
- Scope preservation
- Clarity for implementers
- Audit and liability implications
