---

## 5. Versioning & Compatibility

- Backwards compatibility is **not guaranteed** across major versions
- CSOs are version-pinned to policy hashes
- Deprecated precedents remain auditable indefinitely

---

## 6. Conformance

An implementation is ECAS-conformant if and only if:
- Role transitions are blocking
- Consent is explicit and logged
- Behavior changes mechanically
- Audit artifacts are immutable
